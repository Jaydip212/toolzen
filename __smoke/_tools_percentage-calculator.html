Percentage Calculator

Available

Work out percentages, percentage change and "what percent of" in either direction.

What are you working out?

Three questions, three answers. The working is shown below every result.

Answer

30%

an increase

In words

15% of 200 is 30.

Formula

(15 ÷ 100) × 200

the same working, written out

How this was worked out

  1. Divide the percentage by 100, then multiply by the value.
  2. Negative numbers are carried through the arithmetic rather than being rejected.
  3. Results are rounded to four decimal places. No rounding happens before the division.

How to use Percentage Calculator

  1. 1Pick which of the three questions you are asking: X% of Y, X is what percent of Y, or the change from X to Y.
  2. 2Type the two numbers. Decimals and negative values are both accepted.
  3. 3Read the answer, the sentence in words, and the formula — all three are shown so you can check the working.
  4. 4Division by zero is refused rather than answered with infinity, and a percentage change from zero is explained as undefined.

Percentage Calculator — frequently asked questions

Why does a percentage change from a negative number use its magnitude?

Going from −200 to −100 is a rise, not a fall, even though the second number is larger in absolute terms. Dividing by the magnitude of the starting value keeps the sign of the answer pointing the right way: +50%.

Is 20% then 10% the same as 30% off?

No — that is 28% off, because the second discount applies to the already-reduced price. Use the Discount Calculator if you need to stack offers.

How many decimal places are shown?

Results are rounded to four decimal places for display. No rounding happens before the division, so the answer is as accurate as the inputs allow.

Does the tool send my numbers anywhere?

No. The arithmetic runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded, logged or stored. You can disconnect from the internet and it will still work.
