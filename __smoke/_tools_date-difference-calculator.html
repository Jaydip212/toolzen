How to use Date Difference Calculator

  1. 1Enter the start and end date. The end date is counted and the start date is not — the convention used by payment terms and delivery estimates.
  2. 2Read the calendar breakdown in years, months and days, and the exact total in days and whole weeks.
  3. 3Check the weekday count: Monday to Friday days and weekend days, which add up to the total.
  4. 4Use the weekday panel to add or subtract working days from the start date, skipping Saturdays and Sundays.

Date Difference Calculator — frequently asked questions

Why is the count one day less than I expected?

The start date is excluded. From Monday to the following Monday is 7 days, not 8 — the same way a 30-day notice period starting on the 1st ends on the 31st.

Are public holidays taken out of the weekday count?

No. Only Saturdays and Sundays are excluded, because public holidays differ by country, state and year. Check those against your own calendar.

Why does the tool say months and years are approximate?

Converting days to months needs an average — 30.436875 days — and a 30-day answer is not the same as one month. The calendar breakdown above the conversion is exact.

What if the second date is earlier than the first?

The tool swaps them, reports the size of the gap, and says plainly that the range was reversed rather than showing a negative number of days.
