Regex Tester

Available

Test regular expressions against sample text and inspect every match and group.

Flags

Enter a pattern to begin

Type a regular expression and some test text. Matches are highlighted as you type, and capture groups are listed individually.

Quick reference

The tokens you will reach for most often.

.any character except a newline
\dany digit, 0–9
\wany word character (letter, digit or underscore)
\sany whitespace character
\D \W \Sthe opposite of the lowercase version
^start of the string, or of a line with the m flag
$end of the string, or of a line with the m flag
*zero or more of the previous token
+one or more of the previous token
?zero or one of the previous token, or makes the previous token lazy
{n,m}between n and m repetitions
[abc]any one of the characters listed
[^abc]any character except those listed
(abc)a capturing group
(?:abc)a non-capturing group
(?<name>abc)a named capturing group
a|beither a or b
\ba word boundary

How to use Regex Tester

  1. 1Type your pattern and tick the flags you need (g, i, m, s, u, y).
  2. 2Paste the sample text to test against — matches are highlighted as you type.
  3. 3Open the match list to see each match, its index and its captured groups.

Regex Tester — frequently asked questions

Which regex engine does this use?

It uses your browser’s native JavaScript RegExp engine, so the results are exactly what JavaScript will do at runtime — including the same syntax and the same limits.

Why does my pattern stop matching after a while?

To keep the page responsive, matching stops after 5,000 matches. If your pattern can match an empty string it is also capped so the loop cannot run forever.

Is my pattern or text sent anywhere?

No. The Regex Tester runs entirely in your browser, so your pattern and sample text never leave your device.

Is the Regex Tester free?

Yes. It is completely free, needs no account, and has no usage limit.
