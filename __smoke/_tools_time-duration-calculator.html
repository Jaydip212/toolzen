How to use Time Duration Calculator

  1. 1Type a duration the way you would say it: 2h 30m, 90m, 1.5h, 1 hour 30 minutes or 02:30:45.
  2. 2Read the same duration in seconds, minutes, hours, days and weeks.
  3. 3Enter a start time to see when the duration ends, including the day roll when it crosses midnight.
  4. 4Enter how many items fit in the duration to get a per-item, per-hour and per-minute rate.

Time Duration Calculator — frequently asked questions

Why is a bare number like 90 refused?

Because 90 could mean 90 seconds, 90 minutes or 90 hours. Guessing would silently give you the wrong answer, so the tool asks you to add a unit instead.

Why is 1:30 refused too?

It could be 1 hour 30 minutes or 1 minute 30 seconds. Write 1h 30m, or use the full 01:30:00 form, and there is no ambiguity.

What happens when the end time crosses midnight?

The clock time is shown with the number of days rolled, such as 00:00 (+1 day). Seconds are kept in the label when the shift is not a whole number of minutes.

Can I subtract a duration as well as add it?

Yes. The same panel shows the start time with the duration taken off, which is useful for working out when something began.
