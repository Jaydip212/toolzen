Lorem Ipsum Generator — frequently asked questions

Is the output real Latin?

It is the traditional Lorem Ipsum placeholder text, which is scrambled Latin and deliberately meaningless. It is meant to fill space, not to be read.

Will I get the same text every time?

No. Each generation is randomised, so pressing Generate again gives you different placeholder text at the same length.

Is the generator doing anything online?

No. The words are generated locally in your browser.

Is the Lorem Ipsum Generator free?

Yes. It is completely free, needs no account, and has no usage limit.
