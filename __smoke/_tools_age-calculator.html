How this was worked out

  • Whole months are counted first, then the leftover days are measured from the clamped month anniversary — 31 January plus one month is 28 February.
  • A reference date landing on the last day of its month counts as a whole month, which is what makes a 29 February birthday come due on 28 February.
  • Dates are read in your own timezone, and the time of day is ignored.

Age Calculator — frequently asked questions

Why is 31 January to 1 March reported as 1 month and 1 day?

Because months have different lengths. The leftover days are measured from the clamped month anniversary — 31 January plus one month is 28 February, and one more day takes you to 1 March.

I was born on 29 February. When is my birthday?

In a non-leap year it falls on 28 February, which is the convention most legal systems use. In a leap year it is the real 29 February.

Does the time of day matter?

No. Dates are read in your own timezone and the time of day is ignored, so the count never shifts by a day because of the clock.

Can I work out the age of something other than a person?

Yes. Any two dates work — a company, a building, a warranty. Nothing here is specific to people.
