How to use GST Calculator

  1. 1Choose whether you are adding GST to a tax-exclusive amount or removing it from a tax-inclusive one.
  2. 2Enter the amount and pick the rate slab — 0%, 5%, 12%, 18% and 28% are all offered, plus the less common ones.
  3. 3Choose the supply type: same state splits the tax into CGST and SGST, different states charge IGST at the full rate.
  4. 4Copy the one-line invoice summary, or read the table showing every slab side by side.

GST Calculator — frequently asked questions

Why is removing GST not just the rate times the amount?

Because the rate applies to the net, not the gross. Taking 18% of a tax-inclusive ₹11,800 gives ₹2,124, which overstates the tax — the correct figure is ₹1,800, from gross × 100 ÷ 118.

Does this tell me the rate for my product?

No. It applies the slab you choose. The correct rate depends on the HSN or SAC code and on notifications that change, so check the item before filing.

When is IGST charged instead of CGST and SGST?

When the supplier and the place of supply are in different states or union territories. Within one state the tax splits into two equal halves.

Is this advice for filing a return?

No. It is arithmetic on the figures you enter. Input tax credit, reverse charge, exemptions and composition schemes are not modelled.
