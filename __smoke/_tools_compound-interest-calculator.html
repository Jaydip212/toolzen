How to use Compound Interest Calculator

  1. 1Enter the starting amount, the annual interest rate and the number of years.
  2. 2Choose how often interest compounds: annually, half-yearly, quarterly, monthly or daily.
  3. 3Add a monthly deposit if you are investing regularly. Deposits are applied at the end of each month.
  4. 4Read the final amount, the interest earned, the effective annual rate and what the total is worth after inflation.

Compound Interest Calculator — frequently asked questions

Why is the effective rate higher than the rate I entered?

Because each period's interest starts earning its own interest. ₹1,00,000 at 12% compounded monthly grows by 12.68% in a year, not 12%.

Are deposits at the start or the end of the month?

The end, which is the conservative assumption. A deposit at the start of the month would earn a little more, and the tool does not claim otherwise.

What does the purchasing power figure mean?

It is what the final amount would buy at today's prices, if inflation ran at the rate you enter for the whole term. It is usually a much smaller number than the balance.

Does this account for tax on the interest?

No. Tax, charges and any change in the rate over the term are all outside this calculation, so treat the result as an illustration rather than a forecast.
